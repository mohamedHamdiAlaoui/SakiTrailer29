# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SAKI TRAILER 29 serves Moroccan fleet operators, logistics companies, transport business owners, and procurement teams comparing new Lecitrailer vehicles or selected used commercial vehicles. These users are often working under time pressure: they need to see available stock, understand vehicle categories, confirm practical specs, and contact the team quickly.

The public website must also support multilingual research in English, French, and Spanish. Customers can browse stock without logging in, then create an account to track orders or manage profile details. Internal staff use the admin area to manage products, catalogues, leads, users, and order status.

## Product Purpose

The site presents SAKI TRAILER 29 SARL as the official Lecitrailer representative in Morocco and turns that authority into action: browse new Lecitrailer trailers, semi-trailers, reefers, curtainsiders, rigids, and related categories; compare used vehicles; submit leads; schedule showroom visits; and move serious buyers toward phone, WhatsApp, email, or account-based follow-up.

Success means buyers trust the business quickly, find the right vehicle path without confusion, and can contact the team from any important page. For staff, success means the catalog and order workflows stay current without needing developer help.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, practical, reliable.

The brand should feel like a working showroom for heavy transport professionals: official enough to carry the Lecitrailer relationship, direct enough for operators making real purchasing decisions, and service-minded enough to support delivery, documentation, and follow-up.

## Anti-references

Do not make this feel like a generic automotive marketplace that hides the dealership authority behind endless undifferentiated cards.

Do not make it feel like a luxury lifestyle car site disconnected from heavy transport work, fleet constraints, and operational urgency.

Do not drift into overanimated SaaS dashboard styling with abstract gradients, dark-purple tech mood, decorative glass, or hard-to-scan listings.

Do not reduce it to a thin brochure site that looks local but fails to expose inventory, product detail, contact paths, and showroom visit actions.

## Design Principles

Official representative first. The Lecitrailer Morocco role should be visible early and repeated through product, service, and showroom moments.

Inventory before decoration. Product imagery, availability, category, status, specs, and contact actions carry the experience. Ornament should support scanning, not compete with the vehicles.

Operator-speed clarity. Buyers should be able to compare vehicle classes, stock type, status, mileage, year, location, and next action in seconds.

Multilingual by default. English, French, and Spanish are first-class experiences. Layouts must tolerate translated strings without truncation, overlap, or awkward wrapping.

Trust lives in details. Structured data, real showroom locations, clear contact methods, accessible forms, and admin data hygiene all contribute to the same promise: this is a serious transport partner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No explicit accessibility target is documented in the codebase yet. Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the working standard for new design work.

Future UI should preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, color contrast, reduced-motion alternatives for GSAP and scrolling effects, meaningful image alt text, resilient form validation, and readable layouts for long translated labels. Color must never be the only indicator for product status, order status, validation, or selected filters.
